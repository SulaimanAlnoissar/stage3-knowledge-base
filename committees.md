# GDG KSU Committees

## Overview

GDG KSU is organized into several committees. Each committee supports a specific part of the club's work, such as technical activities, media, events, finance, public relations, human resources, and project management.

## Club Supervision

The club is supervised by Dr. Abdulrhman AlKhnaifer.

## Main Committees

### Technical Committee

The Technical Committee focuses on technical projects, workshops, and activities related to programming, development, artificial intelligence, and other technology topics.

### Media Committee

The Media Committee manages the club's media content, announcements, visual identity, and social media presence.

### Events Committee

The Events Committee plans and organizes club events, workshops, sessions, and student activities.

### Finance Committee

The Finance Committee supports financial planning and manages financial needs related to club activities.

### Public Relations Committee

The Public Relations Committee manages communication with external organizations, partners, and guests.

### Human Resources Committee

The Human Resources Committee supports member organization, internal coordination, and team-related matters.

### Project Management Committee

The Project Management Committee helps organize projects, track progress, and coordinate project teams.
