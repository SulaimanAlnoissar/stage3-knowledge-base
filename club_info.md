# GDG KSU Club Information

## Club Name

GDG KSU stands for Google Developer Group at King Saud University.

## Club Description

GDG KSU is a student club for aspiring developers. The club connects theory with practice through hands-on workshops and collaborative projects. It helps members build solutions for real-world challenges.

## Club Values

GDG KSU is not only a space for working on projects. It is also a community built on clear values.

The club focuses on:

- Continuous learning
- Collaboration
- Initiative
- Building real impact
- Creating a stronger and more inspiring technical community

## Main Focus

The club supports students by providing technical activities, workshops, and project-based learning opportunities.

## Target Audience

The club mainly targets students who are interested in development, technology, artificial intelligence, and building practical projects.
