# GDG KSU Activities

## Overview

GDG KSU organizes and participates in different activities that help students learn, connect with professionals, and build practical technical skills.

The club activities include company visits, technical workshops, in-person sessions, and community initiatives.

## Company Visits

### Saudi Azm and B-IT Visit

GDG KSU members visited Saudi Azm and B-IT.

During the visit, members learned about:

- Work environments
- Professional experiences
- Technical aspects inside the industry
- Real-world practices in the technology sector

The club thanked both companies for their hospitality and valuable participation.

## Technical Workshops

### Vibe Coding Workshop

GDG KSU organized a workshop titled "Vibe Coding".

The workshop discussed:

- The concept of Vibe Coding
- How artificial intelligence can improve the programming experience
- How to choose suitable tools and methods while coding with AI

The workshop also included interaction and participation from attendees.
